Most probes test behavior that differs from what most applications do. Some examples:
- The "Still loading…" line, the filter's 250 ms delay and the delete notice's 8000 ms are each probed on both sides of the limit.
- With the Ready column failed, the count line says "20 cards".
- Arrow keys skip an empty column when moving focus, but not when carrying a card.
- A card dropped under a filter goes directly after the visible card above it.
- A failed bulk move puts the card back at its old index and selects it again.
- Undo stops working once the notice ends, and Control+Z inside a text field does nothing.
- A blank title draft left with Tab is dropped without a message.
- Escape on a card opened from the board goes Back; on a card reached by a typed address, it adds a new history entry.

**Gaps:**
- **Assignee:** no probe covers it. Labels and description are covered.
- **Reload:** no probe covers it. A reload keeps the filter in the address, but the service double also resets the data, so I left it out.
- **Retry into a full column:** no probe covers it. I couldn't find a way to fill In progress without the new notice replacing the error notice, so there was never a Retry button to press.

**Checker limitation:** the checker's column parser can't see SL-117. Its title has a colon, so its accessibility line is quoted. SL-117 is left out of every `order` check, but the answers still name it. In PRB-022 I check the In progress count line ("3 cards") instead of its order.
